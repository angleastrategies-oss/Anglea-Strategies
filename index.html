Common questions

Is GMB the same as Google Business Profile?

Yes. Google renamed Google My Business (GMB) to Google Business Profile. It is the listing that shows up on Google Maps and in the map results at the top of a local search.

How long until I see results?

Profile fixes often show up in calls and direction requests within a few weeks. SEO for your website builds over a few months. We report on both every month so you can see the trend.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. Ongoing work is month to month. Websites and apps are quoted as a project before we start.

Do you only work with businesses in Montana?

We are based in Hamilton and focus on local businesses in the Bitterroot and western Montana, but we work with service businesses anywhere.

Will I own my website and app?

Yes. You own your domain, your content, your Google profile, and the code we build for you.
